Savings Calculator: Monthly Contributions, Interest & Future Value

Use this savings calculator to estimate how an initial balance and regular monthly contributions can grow over time. Enter the starting balance, monthly deposit, annual interest rate and savings period.

How savings growth is estimated

The model combines the future value of the starting balance with the future value of regular monthly contributions using a monthly compound rate.

Contributions vs investment growth

The result separates the amount contributed from the estimated growth, helping you see how much of the final balance comes from deposits versus compounding.

Real-world savings differences

Actual accounts may use different compounding schedules, promotional rates, fees or deposit timing. Use the account's terms for an exact projection.
