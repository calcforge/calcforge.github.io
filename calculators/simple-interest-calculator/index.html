Simple Interest Formula, Examples & Calculation Guide

This simple interest calculator estimates the interest earned on an original principal without compounding. Enter the principal, annual rate and time period to calculate both the interest amount and final balance.

Simple interest formula

The basic formula is I = P × r × t. The final amount is A = P + I. The annual rate is converted from a percentage to a decimal before calculation.

When simple interest is useful

Simple interest is a useful model for situations where interest is calculated only on the original principal or where a simple estimate is required.

Simple interest limitations

Actual financial products may use daily balances, compounding, fees or other contract rules. Check the product terms when an exact amount is required.
