Compound Interest Formula, Examples & How It Works

Use this compound interest calculator to estimate future value and interest earned when returns are added to the balance and compounded over time. Enter the starting principal, annual interest rate, compounding frequency and number of years. The result is a planning estimate based on regular compounding.

How compound interest is calculated

The standard model is A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt), where P is the starting principal, r is the annual rate, n is the number of compounding periods per year and t is time in years.

Compound interest vs simple interest

Simple interest applies the rate to the original principal. Compound interest also applies the rate to previously accumulated interest, so the difference becomes more noticeable over longer periods or at higher rates.

What to check before using the result

Use the actual rate and compounding schedule for the account or investment. Taxes, fees, deposits, withdrawals and changing rates can make a real account differ from this simplified estimate.
