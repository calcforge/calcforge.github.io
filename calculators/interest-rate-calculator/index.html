Interest Rate Formula & Annualized Growth Guide

This interest rate calculator estimates the annualized compound rate implied by a starting amount, ending amount and elapsed time. It is useful for comparing the growth rate represented by two values.

Interest rate formula

For annual compounding, the estimated rate is r = (A/P)^(1/t) − 1, where P is the starting amount, A is the ending amount and t is the number of years.

What an annualized rate means

An annualized rate converts growth over a multi-year period into a single yearly rate under the model's compounding assumption.

Important limitations

The calculation assumes one starting amount and one ending amount. Deposits, withdrawals, fees and irregular cash flows can change the implied rate.
