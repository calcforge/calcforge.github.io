Compound Growth Formula & Future Value Guide

Use this compound growth calculator to project how a starting amount changes when a fixed annual growth rate is applied repeatedly. It is useful for simple long-term growth scenarios.

Compound growth formula

The basic model is FV = PV(1 + r)^t. PV is the present value, r is the annual growth rate and t is the number of years.

Why time changes the result

Because each period's growth becomes part of the next period's base, longer periods can produce increasingly larger differences between linear and compounded growth.

Model assumptions

This calculator models one starting amount. Recurring contributions, withdrawals, taxes, fees and changing rates require a more detailed cash-flow model.
