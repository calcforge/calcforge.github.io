Future Value Formula, Examples & Growth Guide

This future value calculator estimates what a present amount could become after a specified annual rate and time period. It models a single starting amount with compound growth.

Future value formula

The basic compound-growth equation is FV = PV(1 + r)^t. PV is the present value, r is the annual rate and t is the number of years.

Future value vs present value

Present value describes the amount today, while future value describes the modeled amount after applying the selected rate for the selected period.

What is not included

The simplified calculation does not include recurring deposits, withdrawals, taxes, investment fees or changes in the annual rate.
