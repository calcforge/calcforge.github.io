Interest Calculator Formula & Compound Interest Guide

Use this interest calculator to estimate interest earned and the final balance for a regular compound-interest model. Change the annual rate, time period or compounding frequency to compare scenarios.

How the interest calculation works

The compound model uses A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt). Interest earned is the calculated final balance minus the starting principal.

Why compounding frequency matters

With the same nominal rate and time period, changing the number of compounding periods changes the amount added to the balance over time.

Use real account terms for final figures

Bank accounts and investments can include fees, taxes, deposits, withdrawals or changing rates. Treat the calculator as a transparent estimate rather than an account statement.
