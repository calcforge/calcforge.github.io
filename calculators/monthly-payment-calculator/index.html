Monthly Payment Calculator: Loan Payment Formula & Examples

Estimate a regular monthly payment for a fixed-rate installment loan using principal, annual interest rate and repayment term. Use the result to compare payment scenarios before checking an actual lender offer.

Monthly payment formula

The standard amortizing-loan formula uses the loan principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments. A zero-interest case reduces to principal divided by the number of payments.

How rate and term change payment

For the same principal, a higher rate generally increases the monthly payment. A longer term can reduce the monthly payment by spreading repayment over more installments, while total interest can increase.

Check total repayment too

A monthly payment is only one part of borrowing cost. Compare total repayment, total interest, APR and fees when evaluating an actual loan.
