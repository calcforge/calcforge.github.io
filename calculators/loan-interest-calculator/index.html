Loan Interest Formula, Monthly Payment & Total Cost

Use this loan interest calculator to estimate total interest on a fixed-rate installment loan. Enter the amount borrowed, annual interest rate and repayment term to see monthly payment, total repayment and estimated interest.

How loan interest is calculated

The calculator uses a standard monthly amortization model. Total estimated interest equals the scheduled payments over the term minus the original principal.

Rate and term effects

A higher rate generally increases interest cost. A longer term can reduce the monthly payment while spreading interest across more payments.

What the estimate excludes

Origination fees, insurance, taxes, penalties and other lender-specific charges are not included unless they are part of the financed principal.
