Mortgage Payoff Calculator: Extra Payments & Interest Savings

Estimate how additional monthly payments can change the payoff period and total interest on a remaining mortgage balance. This calculator is useful for comparing a regular payment with scenarios that include an extra amount each month.

How extra payments affect a mortgage

An additional payment can reduce principal faster. When the balance falls sooner, future interest is calculated on a smaller balance under the amortization model.

Compare payoff scenarios

Test the current balance, interest rate, remaining term and extra payment amount. Compare the resulting payoff time and estimated interest with the regular-payment scenario.

What the calculator does not include

Actual mortgage contracts can have escrow, fees, prepayment rules and lender-specific calculation methods. Verify the exact payoff amount with the lender before making a financial decision.
