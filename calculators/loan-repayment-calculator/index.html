Loan Repayment Formula & Monthly Payment Guide

Use this loan repayment calculator to estimate the regular monthly payment for a fixed-rate installment loan. Compare the payment and total interest when you change the amount, rate or repayment term.

Loan repayment formula

The standard payment formula is Payment = P × [r(1+r)^n] ÷ [(1+r)^n − 1], where P is principal, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of payments.

Compare repayment terms

Changing the loan term changes both the monthly payment and total interest. Compare scenarios using the same principal and rate so the effect of the term is clear.

Check the lender's final schedule

This calculator is a simplified planning model. Fees, variable rates, irregular payments and lender-specific rules can change the contractual repayment amount.
