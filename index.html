Free calculators for practical work

CalcForge is a professional calculator platform for engineers, electricians, HVAC technicians, contractors, students and people comparing everyday finance scenarios. Each live tool explains its formula, inputs, examples and assumptions so the result can be reviewed instead of treated as a black box.

Electrical tools cover conduit fill, voltage drop, wire size, ampacity, wire gauge, breaker sizing, transformer sizing and three-phase power. Finance tools cover mortgage, loans, auto finance, payoff, amortization, compound interest, savings and future value. Use the tools as preliminary aids and verify critical engineering designs against applicable codes, standards, manufacturer data and project requirements.
