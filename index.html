Free engineering calculators for practical work

CalcForge is a professional calculator platform for engineers, electricians, HVAC technicians, contractors, students and technical teams. Use the library for quick electrical calculations, airflow estimates, mechanical design checks and construction quantity calculations. Each live tool explains its formula and assumptions so the result can be reviewed instead of treated as a black box.

The calculator library is organized into focused topical clusters. Electrical tools cover conductor sizing, voltage drop, conduit fill, ampacity, wire gauge, transformer sizing, breaker sizing and three-phase power. HVAC tools cover airflow and room-volume calculations; mechanical tools cover bearings and rotating equipment; construction tools cover material and building calculations. Category pages and related-tool links make the site easy for both users and search engines to navigate.
