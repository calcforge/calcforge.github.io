Engineering calculator categories

Free engineering calculators for practical work

CalcForge is a professional calculator platform for engineers, electricians, HVAC technicians, contractors, students and technical teams. Use the library for quick electrical calculations, airflow estimates, mechanical design checks and construction quantity calculations. Each live tool explains its formula and assumptions so the result can be reviewed instead of treated as a black box.

Electrical searches often start with a conduit fill calculator, voltage drop calculator, wire size calculator, cable size calculator or wire sizing calculations. CalcForge connects those related tasks with ampacity, wire gauge, breaker sizing, transformer sizing and three-phase power tools. HVAC tools cover airflow and room-volume calculations; mechanical tools cover bearings and rotating equipment; construction tools cover material and building calculations. Category pages and related-tool links make the site easy for both users and search engines to navigate.

Use each calculator as a transparent preliminary engineering aid: review the inputs, formula, worked example and limitations, then verify critical designs against the applicable code, standard, manufacturer data and project requirements.
