Roth IRA Calculator

Learn how Roth IRA contributions and compound growth work and how a calculator estimates future value.

1. What is a Roth IRA?

A Roth IRA is a retirement account funded with after-tax contributions. Qualified withdrawals can receive tax-free treatment under applicable rules. Eligibility, limits and withdrawal rules depend on current law.

2. How the projection works

A projection combines starting balance, recurring contributions, assumed annual return and time. Compounding lets earlier contributions participate in growth for longer.

3. Example

Starting with $5,000 and adding $500 monthly for 20 years at an assumed 7% annual return produces a projected balance made up of both contributions and investment growth. Actual returns will vary.

4. Important assumptions

Check contribution frequency, return assumptions, fees and inflation. Do not treat a constant-return projection as a guaranteed investment outcome.

5. Planning use

Compare different contribution levels and time horizons, then review the current rules for the account before acting.
