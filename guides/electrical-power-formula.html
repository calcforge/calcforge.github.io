Electrical Power Formula

Learn the main electrical power formulas, including P = VI, P = I²R and P = V²/R.

Use the electrical power calculator

1. Basic power

For a simple circuit, P = V × I. Power is measured in watts.

2. Using resistance

For resistive loads, substitution with Ohm's Law gives P = V²/R and P = I²R.

3. Example

A 120 V resistive load drawing 10 A consumes approximately 1,200 W.

4. AC power factor

For single-phase AC real power, a common relationship is P = V × I × PF. Balanced three-phase real power commonly uses √3 × VLL × I × PF.

5. Watts versus VA

Watts describe real power while VA describes apparent power. Power factor links the two.
