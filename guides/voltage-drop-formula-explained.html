Voltage Drop Formula Explained

Understand voltage drop, conductor resistance, circuit length and voltage-drop percentage.

Use the voltage drop calculator

Enter your values below and calculate a live result without leaving this guide.

1. Basic formula

For a simplified resistive circuit, Vd = I × R, where Vd is voltage drop, I is current and R is circuit resistance.

2. Percentage drop

Voltage-drop percentage = Vd ÷ source voltage × 100. A 3 V drop from 120 V is 2.5%.

3. Circuit length

Longer conductors generally create more resistance. Two-wire circuits normally require the complete current path, not just one-way distance.

4. AC and three-phase

AC voltage drop can require impedance and power factor; three-phase systems use a different relationship from single-phase circuits.

5. Wire sizing

Voltage drop is only one selection factor; ampacity, temperature rating, installation method and applicable requirements also matter.
