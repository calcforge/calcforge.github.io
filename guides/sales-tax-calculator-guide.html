1. Sales tax formula

For taxable price P and decimal tax rate t, tax = P × t and final price = P × (1+t).

2. Example

An $80 purchase at 7.5% tax has $6 tax and an $86 total.

3. Tax-inclusive prices

If a displayed total already includes tax, divide the total by 1 plus the tax rate to estimate the pre-tax price.

4. Jurisdiction differences

Rates, exemptions and taxable products vary by jurisdiction. A general calculator cannot determine the legally applicable rate.

5. Common mistakes

Use 0.075 when a calculator expects a decimal rate, and verify the actual local rate before invoicing or filing.
