Three-Phase Power Formula

Learn three-phase power formulas for real power, apparent power, current and power factor.

Use the live 3 phase power calculator

1. Three-phase basics

Three-phase systems use three AC waveforms separated by 120 electrical degrees and are common in industrial power.

2. Real power

For a balanced system, P = √3 × VLL × I × PF.

3. Apparent power

S = √3 × VLL × I, expressed in VA or kVA.

4. Current from power

I = P ÷ (√3 × VLL × PF). Keep units consistent when using kW.

5. Example

At 400 V, 20 A and 0.85 PF, real power is about 11.8 kW under the balanced-system assumption.

Practical takeaway

Use these formulas to understand the calculation and compare scenarios. Verify current legal, tax, engineering-code and project-specific requirements before using an estimate for a real decision.
