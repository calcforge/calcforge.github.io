Discount Calculator

Learn how to calculate percentage discounts, sale prices and the amount saved.

1. Discount formula

For original price P and decimal discount d, discount amount = P × d and sale price = P × (1−d).

2. Example

A $120 item at 25% off saves $30, leaving a sale price of $90.

3. Successive discounts

Two discounts applied sequentially are multiplicative, not simply additive. A 20% discount followed by 10% leaves 72% of the original price.

4. Reverse calculation

If sale price and discount are known, original price = sale price ÷ (1−discount rate).

5. Discount versus markup

A percentage discount from a selling price and a percentage markup from cost use different bases and should not be treated as opposites.
