AWG Wire Gauge Explained

Learn how American Wire Gauge relates to conductor size, resistance, ampacity and practical wire selection.

1. What is AWG?

American Wire Gauge is a standardized wire-size numbering system. Smaller AWG numbers represent larger conductors.

2. Gauge and resistance

A larger conductor generally has lower resistance for a given material and length.

3. AWG is not ampacity

Allowable ampacity depends on conductor material, insulation, temperature rating, installation method and applicable requirements.

4. Voltage drop

Long runs and high current can make voltage drop a limiting factor even when ampacity is adequate.

5. Selection

Final conductor selection should satisfy ampacity, voltage-drop targets, terminal ratings and governing electrical requirements.
