Power Factor Explained

Understand real power, apparent power, reactive power and why power factor matters in AC systems.

1. Definition

Power factor is commonly defined as PF = kW ÷ kVA for AC systems.

2. Why it matters

For a given real power, lower power factor generally means higher current and greater loading of conductors and equipment.

3. Power triangle

Real power is measured in W or kW, apparent power in VA or kVA and reactive power in VAR or kVAR.

4. Three-phase relationship

For a balanced system, S = √3 × VLL × I and real power is related to apparent power through PF.

5. Correction

Inductive loads can have lagging power factor. Correction systems must be designed for the actual installation.

Practical takeaway

Use these formulas to understand the calculation and compare scenarios. Verify current legal, tax, engineering-code and project-specific requirements before using an estimate for a real decision.
