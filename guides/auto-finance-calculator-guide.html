Auto Finance Calculator

Learn how auto finance calculations estimate monthly payments, total interest and vehicle financing cost.

1. Amount financed

The amount financed can differ from the vehicle price because down payments, trade-ins, taxes, fees and add-ons affect the balance.

2. Payment formula

A fixed-rate installment payment can be estimated with M = P × r(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is principal, r is periodic rate and n is number of payments.

3. Example

For a $25,000 loan at 6% annual interest over 60 months, convert the annual rate to 0.06/12 and apply the amortization formula.

4. Total cost

Always compare total repayment and interest, not just the monthly payment. Longer terms can reduce the scheduled payment while increasing interest.

5. Real-world charges

Taxes, registration, dealer fees, insurance and optional products may change the actual financing cost.
