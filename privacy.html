Privacy policy

CalcForge is designed so calculator inputs and calculations run in your browser.

Calculator inputs

Calculator values are processed client-side by the website's JavaScript. CalcForge does not need a server account to perform the calculations.

Analytics

CalcForge uses Google Analytics 4 to understand page views, calculator article views and site usage. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may process information such as pages viewed, device/browser information, approximate geographic information and interaction events according to Google's applicable policies.

Third-party services

The site may use third-party services such as Google Analytics and advertising providers. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies or similar technologies.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site's features, analytics or advertising practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
