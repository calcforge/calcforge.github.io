Engineering calculator disclaimer

CalcForge provides preliminary calculation tools for information, estimating and educational use.

Not a substitute for professional design

Calculator outputs are not engineering certification, code approval, inspection approval or a guarantee of safe performance. Critical electrical, HVAC, mechanical and construction decisions should be reviewed by a suitably qualified professional.

Check assumptions and inputs

Results depend on the values entered and on the simplified assumptions described on each page. Real projects can require additional factors such as temperature, installation method, conductor construction, power factor, loading, material properties, tolerances, environmental conditions and local requirements.

Codes and standards

References to NEC or other standards are provided as engineering context only. Codes and standards change, local jurisdictions may adopt different editions, and project-specific rules may apply. Verify final calculations against the current adopted requirements and manufacturer data.
