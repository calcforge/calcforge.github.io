ABOUT CALCFORGE

Engineering tools built for practical work

CalcForge is a free, client-side library of engineering and professional calculators designed to make common calculations faster to check and easier to understand.

Our approach

Each calculator is designed around a clear input, formula and result workflow. Where a calculation depends on code requirements, installation conditions or manufacturer data, the page explains those limitations instead of presenting a simplified result as universal engineering approval.

Who the tools are for

The library is intended for engineers, electricians, HVAC professionals, contractors, technicians, students and anyone who needs a quick preliminary calculation. The tools can support estimating, design review and learning, but they do not replace a licensed professional, applicable code or project-specific engineering judgment.

What we prioritize

CalcForge focuses on useful search intent, transparent formulas, worked examples, accessible interfaces, fast loading and strong internal links between related calculations. The goal is a reference library people can return to, not a collection of thin pages made only for search traffic.
